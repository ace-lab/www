## Preparing your poster for printing on a plotter

1. In PowerPoint, define a custom paper size of 40″ **wide** by 30″ **tall**. Other sizes are not easily supported, so your poster **must** be these dimensions.
2. Create a single large slide with those dimensions.
3. Print to PDF or Save As PDF a single page document containing that slide. Open the document in Preview, Acrobat, or whatever to verify that it opens as a landscape-format single page of dimensions 40″w x 30″h.
4. Upload **both** the PDF and the original ppt/pptx file (just in case) to the designated folder. Other source formats (Google Slides, Figma, etc) aren’t supported; if you use one of these to make a PDF, it’s your responsibility to make sure the PDF is the right size and format.

## CITI Training {#citi}

We often do human-subjects experiments to evaluate our work. All students involved in handling data from such experiments must complete basic online [CITI training](http://cphs.berkeley.edu/training.html). Go through the registration and login process, and then take the course “Group 2 Social Behavioral Human Research”. When you complete it, keep the email that contains the proof that you passed (a link to a PDF Certificate of Completion), and contact your faculty advisor to have your certificate added to [this folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Xc33Rh9neqC31x6iHdQBqCG9roQvvycE), so it can be easily located when we apply for new protocols.

## Card key access to BiD Lab

You will first need to complete a bit of required safety training—the cardkey form will ask you to upload the files showing completion of these brief online modules, which you can [search for here](https://ehs.berkeley.edu/training):

- EHS 101 – Fundamentals of Laboratory Safety
- EHS 207 – UC Berkeley Guidelines on Protecting Workers From COVID-19 (revised 7/7/21)
- EHS 502 – Workplace Safety Program

The current Lab Safety/Orientation person for the BiD Lab is Timothy J. Aveni <tja@berkeley.edu>. There is a brief orientation you’ll need to go through with them. You’ll need to attest this on your cardkey access form.

Once you’ve done the above, you’ll be given a link to a form that will provide you with keycard access.

## Registering for conference travel

It’s often easier to get your registration and travel arrangements made directly and charged to the appropriate grant. Ask your advisor.

If you are writing papers about work supported by the mastery-based learning grant, please acknowledge them using this language. Include “in part” if your work was also supported by other funds (fellowship, GSR, etc.)

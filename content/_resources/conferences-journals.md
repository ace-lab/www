Some of the venues we typically follow and submit our work to…

### Conferences/workshops sorted by typical deadlines

- **[SIGCSE Technical Symposium (Special Interest Group on Computer Science Education)](https://sigcse2025.sigcse.org/)**: Abstracts due mid-July; full papers due late July.
- **[ITiCSE (Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education)](https://iticse.acm.org/)**: Abstracts due early January; full papers due mid-January.
- **[ICER (International Computing Education Research Conference)](https://icer.acm.org/)**: Abstracts due early April; full papers due mid-April.
- **[CHI (Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems)](https://chi2025.acm.org/)**: Papers due mid-September.
- **[WiPSCE (Workshop in Primary and Secondary Computing Education)](https://www.wipsce.org/)**: Abstracts due late April; full papers due early May.
- **[Koli Calling (International Conference on Computing Education Research)](https://www.kolicalling.fi/)**: Abstracts due early August; full papers due late August.
- **[CompEd (ACM Global Computing Education Conference)](https://comped.acm.org/2025/call-for-submissions/)**: Abstracts due March 17, 2025; full papers due March 24, 2025.
- **[SPLASH-E (Software and Programming Languages Education)](https://2025.splashcon.org/track/splash-2025-SPLASH-E)**: Please refer to the official conference website for submission deadlines.
- **[ACE (Australasian Computing Education Conference)](https://ace2025.org/)**: Abstracts due mid-September; full papers due early October.
- **[AIEd (Artificial Intelligence in Education)](https://aied2025.org/)**: Abstracts due late January; full papers due early February.
- **[EDM (Educational Data Mining)](https://educationaldatamining.org/edm2025/)**: Abstracts due early February; full papers due mid-February.
- **[FIE (Frontiers in Education)](https://fie2025.org/)**: Abstracts due early February; full papers due late February.
- **[L@S (Learning at Scale)](https://learningatscale.acm.org/las2025/)**: Abstracts due early January; full papers due mid-January.
- **[LWMOOCS (Learning with MOOCs)](https://lwmoocs2025.org/)**: Abstracts due mid-April; full papers due late April.
- **[UIST (User Interface Software and Technology)](https://uist.acm.org/uist2025/)**: Abstracts due early May; full papers due mid-May.
- **[ICSE (International Conference on Software Engineering)](https://conf.researchr.org/home/icse-2025)**: Abstracts due mid-September; full papers due late September.
- **[ASEE (American Society for Engineering Education)](https://www.asee.org/annual-conference/2025)**: Abstracts due early October; full papers due late October.
- **[ISLS (International Society of the Learning Sciences)](https://www.isls.org/conferences/)**: Abstracts due mid-November; full papers due late November.
- PLATEAU Workshop on the Intersection of HCI and PL, hosted at Berkeley 2024: Feb 19-20; papers Dec 6
- Symp. on Visual Languages and Human-Centric Computing (VL/HCC)
